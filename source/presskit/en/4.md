**Lead**

Vienna duo WUHA blends synthpop, funk and soul into music for the night drive and the dance floor. With their latest single “Losing My Shape” the band takes the next step – their songs are already heard in nine countries.

**Body**

Vienna, \[DATE\] – Warm synths, a singing guitar and choruses that stay until morning: WUHA make synthpop with an 80s soul. Behind the project are Wolfgang Laab and Andreas Wukics-Hagen, both singers, songwriters and producers. They met as teenagers, founded their first band Cinderella together and later toured Austria, Germany and Switzerland with the Top 40 band Broadway.

Andreas worked for many years as a sound engineer and mixer for TV and cinema and founded lifestyle-tv.com. Wolfgang Laab studied at the Musicians Institute in Hollywood, wrote the Austrian Top 40 hit “Neue Helden” and has led Alf Poier’s band since 2010. His guitar solo on “Rainbow Eyes” is a highlight of the WUHA catalog.

Since the debut “Caroline (feat. Marc Voc)” in February 2025 – a song first written in 1989 – WUHA has released six singles and club mixes. Radio stations such as Breaking Sound Radio (USA), CragsRadio and Chic Radio Hits play the band, and their singles are on 70 Spotify playlists. Listeners are in Italy, the USA, the Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia, Austria, Spain, Brazil and Colombia.

“Losing My Shape” came out on 28 August 2026. The song is about the fragile moment when dreams, memories and connections slowly fade – recorded with just a few musicians in a small studio room in Purkersdorf near Vienna. WUHA is now working on their first album.

**About WUHA (boilerplate)**

WUHA is a synthpop act from Vienna, Austria. Wolfgang Laab and Andreas Wukics-Hagen mix pop and dance with funk, soul, rock and the sound of the 80s. Motto: Feel the Groove. www.wuha.world
