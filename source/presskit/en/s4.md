## About the song

“Caroline” began as a teenage crush in Vienna’s nightlife of the late 80s. Andreas Wukics-Hagen wrote the song in 1989. It tells of a love that was never spoken – inspired by short encounters at the legendary Vienna club Poeblo. Back then it quickly became a favorite of his band Staccato and caught attention at the Austrian band contest Popodrom.

In 2024 a week in COVID quarantine became the spark to bring the song back. Newly produced, with session singer Marc Voc and Wolfgang Laab on guitar, it came out in February 2025 as WUHA’s debut single. Radio stations in France, Italy, the UK and the USA played it. In April 2025 the Extended Club Mix followed for the dance floor – a piece of the past in the sound of today.
