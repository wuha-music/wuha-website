## One-line bio

WUHA is a synthpop act from Vienna, Austria: music for the night drive and the dance floor – with an 80s soul, warm synths and a lot of groove.

## Short bio (approx. 60 words)

WUHA from Vienna mixes synthpop, pop and dance with funk, soul and rock. The sound takes you back to the 80s – neon lights, big city, longing – and still feels modern. WUHA are Wolfgang Laab and Andreas Wukics-Hagen, both singers, songwriters and producers. Since 2025 they have released six singles and club mixes, played on radio from Mexico to Los Angeles.

## Long bio (approx. 250 words)

Neon lights on a wet street, the last drive through the city, a chorus that stays until morning: this is the world of WUHA. The Vienna duo makes synthpop with an 80s soul – built on funk, soul and rock, made for the night and the dance floor.

Andreas Wukics-Hagen and Wolfgang Laab met as teenagers. Together they founded their first band, Cinderella, and later toured Austria, Germany and Switzerland with the Top 40 band Broadway. Then their paths went different ways – in 2025 they came back together as WUHA, joining their analog roots with modern production.

Wolfgang Laab is a musician, composer and lyricist. After Broadway and tours across Western Europe – sharing the stage with the E Street Band and The Platters, among others – he studied at the Musicians Institute in Hollywood in 1996. Later he worked in the USA with Peter Wolf (Frank Zappa, Commodores) as an engineer and musician. Back in Vienna he wrote and produced for other artists: his song “Neue Helden” entered the Austrian Top 40 at number 13 in 2007. He wrote lyrics and vocal lines for the Austrian TV series “Schnell ermittelt”, sang in the Falco film “Verdammt, wir leben noch!” and has been bandleader for Austrian comedian and musician Alf Poier since 2010.

Andreas Wukics-Hagen has been on stage since the age of six – for many years together with his father. With Cinderella he won a band contest and recorded the records “Loving You” and “To Be a Star”; with his band Staccato he first played “Caroline” in 1989. Later he worked as a sound engineer and re-recording mixer for Austrian TV and cinema, and founded lifestyle-tv.com.

WUHA’s debut single “Caroline (feat. Marc Voc)” came out in 2025 and was played by radio stations in France, Italy, the UK and the USA. Today WUHA has listeners in Italy, the USA, the Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia, Austria, Spain, Brazil and Colombia. The band is now working on its first album.

## The musicians

- **Wolfgang Laab** – vocals, guitar, songwriting, production. Guitar solo on “Rainbow Eyes”, guitar on “Caroline”. Studied at the Musicians Institute, Hollywood; worked with Peter Wolf (USA); Austrian Top 40 hit “Neue Helden” (2007, for Gernot Pachernigg); lyrics and vocals for “Schnell ermittelt” and the Falco film “Verdammt, wir leben noch!”; bandleader for Alf Poier since 2010. More: www.wolfganglaab.at
- **Andreas Wukics-Hagen** – vocals, musician, songwriting, production. On stage since age six, writer of “Caroline” (1989, band Staccato). Later sound engineer and mixer for TV and cinema, founder of lifestyle-tv.com.
- **Together:** first band Cinderella (won a band contest, records “Loving You” and “To Be a Star”), then Top 40 band Broadway with tours of Austria, Germany and Switzerland. WUHA since 2025.

Guests: Petra Suk (“Suki”, from Two In One) – backing vocals on “Rainbow Eyes”. Marc Voc – session singer on “Caroline”.
