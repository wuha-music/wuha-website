## About the song

“Nothing Is Forever” is an anthem about impermanence – and WUHA’s most rock-driven single: 132 BPM, strong guitars, a driving beat. The song moves through the highs and lows of life – love, laughter, pain and silence, sunshine and rain. Its message: because nothing lasts, every moment counts. Andreas Wukics-Hagen dedicated the song to his late father, with whom he performed on stage for many years.
