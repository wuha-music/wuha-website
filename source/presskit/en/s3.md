## About the song

“Rainbow Eyes” is a pop song with an 80s soul: warm synths, a big chorus and a guitar solo by Wolfgang Laab. On backing vocals: Petra Suk, known as “Suki” from the duo Two In One. Six months later came the EDM Club Mix – the same melody, built for the dance floor. The original fits daytime radio, the club mix evening and dance shows.
