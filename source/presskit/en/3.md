# TV

For music, culture and magazine shows and regional TV.

## Why WUHA for TV

WUHA comes with strong images: neon, night, big city, black and white with pink light. And with stories you can tell in two minutes.

## Story ideas

1. **From the mixing desk to the microphone.** For years Andreas mixed sound for Austrian TV and cinema. Now he is the artist – and the camera is on him.
2. **A song from 1989.** “Caroline” was written in 1989 in Vienna’s nightlife, inspired by Club Poeblo. In 2024 a week in quarantine brought it back. Today it plays on radio in France, Italy, the UK and the USA.
3. **Teenage friends, together again.** Andreas and Wolfgang started their first band as teenagers and toured Europe with Broadway. Decades later they are back together as WUHA.
4. **Two Vienna music pros.** Wolfgang Laab studied in Hollywood, worked with Peter Wolf, wrote a Top 40 hit, sang in the Falco film and leads Alf Poier’s band. With WUHA he now makes synthpop for the world.
5. **Vienna – Jakarta – Manila.** A band from Vienna whose most loyal fans are in Indonesia, the Philippines, Spain and the USA. How music travels in the streaming age.
6. **Behind the scenes.** How do you make an 80s sound today? A look behind the scenes of the production: synths, guitar, vocals.
