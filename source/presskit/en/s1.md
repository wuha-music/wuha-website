## About the song

“Losing My Shape” is WUHA’s latest single. It is about the fragile moment when dreams, memories and human connections slowly fade. It speaks of uncertainty, loneliness and the search for something to hold on to – and of the wish to keep what matters. It was recorded in an intimate setting, with just a few musicians in a small studio room in Purkersdorf near Vienna. Written by Andreas Wukics-Hagen, Wolfgang Laab and Karen Fellinger.
