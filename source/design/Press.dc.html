Short bio · 50 words

WUHA is a synthpop duo from Vienna, Austria. Andreas and Wolfgang make music for the late-night drive and the dance floor: glowing synths, 80s nostalgia and roots in funk, soul and rock. Their singles have been added to 70 Spotify playlists and played on radio from Mexico to Los Angeles.

Long bio

The Viennese production team WUHA unites a love for music with a shared history that began on stage. As teenagers, Andreas and Wolfgang met in a music store and founded their first band, Cinderella. Later, with their Top-40 band Broadway, they toured Germany, Austria, Italy and Switzerland.

Their paths went different ways: Wolfgang moved to the U.S. and worked in studios alongside producers like Peter Wolf, while Andreas became a sound engineer for Austrian television and cinema. In 2025 they reunited as WUHA. The analog soul of their roots meets modern production. Feel the Groove.
