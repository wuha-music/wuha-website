The story behind

Born in a small studio room

"Losing My Shape" reflects the fragile moment when dreams, memories and human connections slowly begin to fade, when everything familiar starts to lose its outline.

Born in a small studio room in Purkersdorf, near Vienna, the song began as an intimate recording with only a few musicians, a quiet space and a powerful emotional atmosphere. What started as a fragile demo has now found its moment to step into the world.

The song takes us on a haunting journey through uncertainty, isolation and the search for direction. Yet within this feeling of being lost lies a deeper human struggle: the desire to hold on, to keep rising from the cold, and to find meaning when everything seems to fade away.
