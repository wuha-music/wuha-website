About WUHA

A shared history that began on stage.

The Viennese production team WUHA unites a love for music with a shared history that began on stage and now continues to shine in a new light. As singers, musicians, songwriters and producers, Wolfgang and Andreas bring together their experience from live performance, studio work and film production to create a truly unique sound.

Raised in Vienna, the capital of classical music, the two met by chance in a music store. The spark was immediate: "Let's rock!" became their motto, and their first band, Cinderella, was born. With an average age of just 17, they played band contests, concerts and open-air festivals. Later, with their professional Top-40 band Broadway, they toured Germany, Austria, Italy, Switzerland and more.

AndreasVocals · Music · Songwriting · ProductionMade a name for himself as a sound engineer in Austrian television and cinema.
WolfgangVocals · Guitar · Songwriting · ProductionMoved to the U.S. and worked in renowned studios alongside producers like Peter Wolf. Plays the guitar solo on "Rainbow Eyes" and the guitar on "Caroline".
2025: the reunionFunk · Soul · RockMatured songs and new visions. The analog soul of their roots meets modern production. Honest, handcrafted music.
