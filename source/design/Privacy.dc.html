3. Collection and processing of personal data

We collect and process your personal data only to the extent necessary to provide a functional website and our content and services.

4. Data collection on the website

4.1 Hosting and server log files

Our website is hosted by world4you Internet Services GmbH, Austria. When you visit our website, information is automatically collected and transmitted by your browser to our server. This includes:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL
  • Hostname of the accessing computer
  • Time of the server request
  • IP address

The processing is carried out on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR for the purpose of ensuring the smooth operation of the website.

4.2 Fonts, images and music files

Fonts, images, covers, audio files and press downloads are stored on our own server. No data is sent to font or content delivery services such as Google Fonts.

4.3 Cookies and cookie settings

Our website uses cookies. Before any cookie or service that is not strictly necessary is used, we ask for your consent in our cookie banner (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 165 (3) TKG 2021). You can choose between these categories:

  • Necessary: stores your cookie choice in the cookie "wuha_consent" for 6 months. Always active.
  • Statistics: Google Analytics (see 5.1).
  • Marketing: Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel (see 5.2 and 5.3).
  • External media: Spotify player on our song pages (see 5.4).

Visitors from the European Union see the cookie banner. For visitors outside the European Union, statistics and marketing are active by default. Everyone can change or revoke the choice at any time with the cookie button on every page or here: open cookie settings. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third-party tools

5.1 Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager

With your consent, our website uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, services provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google Tag Manager is only loaded after you agree to statistics or marketing. Google Analytics uses cookies that enable analysis of your website usage. The information generated by the cookie is usually transferred to a Google server and stored there. IP addresses are shortened. We use Google Consent Mode, so Google receives your consent choice.

Data processing is based on your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). You can revoke your consent at any time. Further information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Meta Pixel

With your consent to marketing, we use the Meta Pixel of Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland, to measure our ads on Facebook and Instagram, for example clicks to Spotify and newsletter sign-ups. Data may be transferred to Meta Platforms, Inc. in the USA. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. Further information: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

5.3 TikTok Pixel

With your consent to marketing, we use the TikTok Pixel of TikTok Technology Limited, 10 Earlsfort Terrace, Dublin, D02 T380, Ireland, to measure our ads on TikTok. Data may be transferred to countries outside the EU. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. Further information: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy-eea

5.4 Spotify, YouTube and other music platforms

Our song pages can show a Spotify player (Spotify AB, Regeringsgatan 19, SE-111 53 Stockholm, Sweden). The player is only loaded when you click "Play here" or when you have allowed external media. Only then data is transmitted to Spotify. Links to YouTube, Apple Music, Amazon Music, SoundCloud and social media open the respective website; data is only transmitted when you follow the link.

Further information: Spotify https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/, YouTube https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Newsletter (MailPoet)

You can subscribe to our newsletter. We collect your email address and your preferred language (English or German). After you sign up, you receive an email to confirm your subscription (double opt-in). The data is stored with the WordPress plugin MailPoet on our own server. Data processing is based on your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email.
